11: ## 1. Executive Summary & Mission Statement
12: 
13: Modern automotive product management requires synthesizing billions of fragmented market signals, customer reviews, warranty reports, competitor actions, and strategic corporate roadmaps into a coherent, customer-facing product specification. Today, this process spans approximately **two years** per vehicle generation and is predominantly manual, fragmented, and vulnerable to subjective bias.
14: 
15: ### The Mission
16: Develop an AI-powered, human-in-the-loop enterprise application that:
17: 1. **Ingests Heterogeneous Data**: Combines internal BMW customer feedback, quantitative satisfaction surveys, vehicle equipment catalogs, and strategic corporate reports.
18: 2. **Extracts Actionable Signals**: Discovers recurring complaints, unmet customer needs, competitor advantages, and emerging market trends, each strictly linked to originating evidence.
19: 3. **Derives SMART Product Requirements**: Formulates clear, customer-facing, realistic, and measurable requirements adhering to SMART criteria.
20: 4. **Prioritizes with Explainable Logic**: Applies an objective, transparent, multi-factor scoring methodology that balances customer value, regional volume impact, corporate strategy, and implementation complexity.
21: 5. **Keeps the Product Manager Firmly in the Loop**: Enables the PM to review, approve, reject, modify, or challenge requirements, rationales, and underlying evidence.
22: 6. **Maintains a Forensic Audit Trail**: Chronologically logs every system derivation, PM intervention, score override, and decision rationale to guarantee end-to-end governance.

106: | Dimension | Weight | Key Evaluation Requirements |

110: | **Traceability & Auditability** | 20% | Full backward lineage from every requirement and score back to originating quotes, surveys, and report slides. Complete, chronological, tamper-evident audit history of all approvals, edits, and rejections. |
111: | **Solution Quality, Usability & Feasibility** | 20% | Prototype UI/UX polish, usability for BMW Product Managers and engineering peers, realistic applicability in an enterprise automotive environment, and quality of SMART formulations. |
112: | **Innovation, Scalability & Business Impact** | 15% | Originality of approach, cross-derivative scalability (F70 $\to$ G60 $\to$ G70 $\to$ Neue Klasse), reduction in manual requirement definition cycle time, and prevention of costly late-stage engineering rework. |

125: ## 5. SMART Requirements Framework
126: 
127: BMW requirements must strictly comply with the **SMART** standard:
128: 
129: | SMART Dimension | Definition | Automotive Context |
130: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
131: | **Specific** | Concrete and unambiguous system or feature scope | Exact UI component, sensor, or interior system |
132: | **Measurable** | Objective engineering/UX KPIs & thresholds | Clear quantifiable metrics (e.g. latency $< 45\text{ms}$, 60 fps) |
133: | **Attainable** | Feasible within vehicle architecture & manufacturing | Compatible with platform packaging & wiring harness |
134: | **Relevant** | Meaningfully solves verified customer pain | Directly resolves top complaint or aligns with brand strategy |
135: | **Timely** | Explicit SOP target & vehicle milestone | SOP 2028 (LCI / Successor / Neue Klasse milestone) |
136: 
137: ### Benchmark Example: From Complaint to SMART Requirement
138: * **Raw Voice of Customer (`G70-0891`)**: *"Customer noted she would have chosen the Home theater screen, but it obstructs the view out the rear and BMW does not offer a camera/rear-view mirror screen."*
139: * **Unacceptable Requirement (Vague)**: *"Improve rear visibility when the theater screen is down."*
140: * **SMART Requirement (`REQ-G70-VIS-001`)**:
141:   * **Specific**: Full-display digital streaming rear-view mirror system utilizing an aerodynamic roof-mounted wide-angle camera feeding directly into the central rear-view mirror.
142:   * **Measurable**: End-to-end video latency $< 45\text{ ms}$; frame rate $\ge 60\text{ fps}$ at $1080\text{p}$; dynamic range $> 120\text{ dB}$ (headlight anti-glare); camera lens auto-defrosting and wash nozzle operational from $-30^\circ\text{C}$ to $+65^\circ\text{C}$.
143:   * **Attainable**: Integrates with the existing Neue Klasse roof shark-fin camera housing and high-speed automotive Ethernet backbone.
144:   * **Relevant**: Eliminates the #1 safety blocker preventing theater screen adoption; projected to increase Theater Screen package take-rate by 18%.
145:   * **Timely**: Targeted for SOP 2028 (G70 LCI / Neue Klasse Luxury derivative).
146:   * **Package Strategy**: Standard on i7 M70; optional in Innovation Package for base derivatives.
